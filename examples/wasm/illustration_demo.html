Geometry

STEP → GLB surface mesh

Chord error: maximum positional gap, in millimetres, between the exact STEP surface and its triangle approximation. Smaller values add triangles on curved geometry; planar faces are mostly unchanged.

Mesh angle: angular deflection allowed while the mesh follows changing surface direction. Smaller angles add triangles where curvature changes and make rounded silhouettes appear smoother.

Both settings rebuild the filled 3D preview and SVG/Canvas surface mesh by reconverting STEP to GLB.

HLR linework mesh

Controls only HLR detail lines and the mesh-shadow outline; it does not change filled surface triangles.

The demo does not impose a triangle-count limit; mesh density is controlled by the STEP and HLR tolerances above.

Surface

Fusion converts safe opaque triangle groups into polygon regions while preserving depth-order boundaries. Coplanar layering paints a continuous base beneath shallow material markings before overpainting their colors. Ambiguous folds, overlaps, and transparent surfaces remain ordered triangles.

Linework

The mesh illustration uses Geometer's fast C++ vector detail plus the separately composable hierarchical mesh-shadow outline. GPU HLR retains its own boundary/crease edge buffer, fills the hardware depth buffer with opaque faces, then draws only edge pixels that pass the depth test. Camera motion does not invoke OCCT or rebuild projected vectors.
