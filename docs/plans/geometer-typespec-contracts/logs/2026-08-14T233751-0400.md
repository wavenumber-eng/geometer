# Log: analytic-planar-boolean-promotion

# Promotion slice 2c: capsule and swept-path lowering

Third sub-slice of slice 2: lowering of geometry kinds 4 (capsule) and 5
(swept path) into exact atomic boundary curves, completing operand
lowering ahead of the batch orchestrator sub-slice. Includes two
correctness fixes to shared exact machinery uncovered by the new
geometry (both flagged below).

## Changes

- `analytic_operand_lowering.{h,cpp}`: capsules lower directly as the
  stadium boundary (two offset segments plus two cap semicircle arc
  pairs); the half-width inflation uses a doubled span accumulator so an
  even width landing exactly on the governed 10^12 nm span limit lowers
  while one more half-unit per side reports `resource_limit_exceeded`.
- Swept paths lower through a job-local pre-arrangement: per-segment
  offset strips and cap/joint disks are emitted as local pieces (with
  construction chords closing each strip strictly inside the vertex
  disks), a new atomization pass (`atomize_swept_pieces`) splits every
  piece curve at pairwise carrier intersections (coincident carriers
  probe the four endpoints; splits ordered per curve by exact travel
  order; sub-arc `major` flags recomputed), the pieces run through
  `build_exact_arrangement`, and `emit_swept_union_boundary` re-emits
  every edge with material on exactly one side as this job's
  occurrences, attributed to the lowest-numbered non-construction local
  occurrence whose piece covers the material side. Construction chords
  may never survive onto the emitted boundary (`invalid_topology` if
  attribution fails).
- Shared machinery fix 1 (`exact_factorization.cpp`): degree-2
  polynomials now factor through a discriminant perfect-square test
  (Newton `integer_sqrt`) instead of the divisor-enumeration search,
  whose trial division blew the work budget for square roots of large
  rationals (governed-span capsule lengths). This lowers governed work
  boundaries: the exact-geometry vector success boundary moves from
  41482728 to 41182568 (updated in `exact_geometry_test.cpp`,
  `docs/contracts/promotion-manifest.toml`, and the manifest pytest),
  and the expression test-internal pins move 3883272 -> 3879048
  (success) and 3494472 -> 3490248 (one-unit-short rollback).
- Shared machinery fix 2 (`exact_arrangement_order.cpp`): the rotational
  order around a vertex is cut at angle 0 (+x axis); a clockwise germ
  whose tangent lies exactly on +x dips infinitesimally below the axis
  and must wrap to the very end of the whole order. `direction_half` now
  takes the germ tangent and assigns that case a virtual half 2, and the
  previous `seam_rank` special case in `compare_curvature` is removed
  (it could not cross the half boundary in `compare_outgoing`, and was
  wrong for `compare_cycle_germs_at_minimum`, whose sweep starts at
  south where angle 0 is mid-sweep). Without the fix the L-shape swept
  union fused three faces through a bad DCEL next-pointer at the
  tangent vertex.
- New tests: capsule lowering geometry and diagnostics (degenerate
  endpoints, span exactly at the governed limit lowers, half-width
  overflow fails); single-segment swept differential against the
  capsule; quarter-arc swept differential; L-shape (tangent-adjacent
  segments); overlapping/tangent/separated U-shape strokes through the
  full union pipeline (separations 8/10/20: two-dimensional leg overlap
  with rational 3-4-5 crossings, exactly coincident offset lines with
  opposite material sides, and disjoint strips); swept invalid-input
  diagnostics. `run_union_pipeline`'s budget rises from 2e9 to 40e9
  work units: the overlapping-U pipeline needs about 28.1e9 measured
  end to end (dominated by O(n^2) atomic pair validation), and the
  limit keeps headroom without masking runaway regressions.

## Interpretation decisions and risks flagged for review

- Two governed shared-machinery files changed (`exact_factorization.cpp`
  degree-2 fast path, `exact_arrangement_order.cpp` rotational wrap
  fix), and a governed manifest pin
  (`geometry_vector_success_work_units`) moved as a direct consequence
  of the factorization fast path.
- Swept overlap resolution happens pre-arrangement inside lowering via
  the new atomization pass; the downstream batch arrangement then sees
  only union-boundary curves for each swept operand.
- Cost scaling: atomic pair validation is O(n^2) over atomized curves at
  roughly 5e6-100e6 work per pair with arc-heavy inputs; a 3-segment
  self-overlapping stroke costs about 28e9 work end to end. Larger
  strokes will need either sweep pruning or budget policy work before
  production dispatch.
- Discovered kernel limitation: result normalization certifies rounded
  arcs with `exact_arc_hausdorff_within`, which takes a square root over
  candidate points that already carry sqrt coordinates; for boundary
  vertices with nested radicals (irrational circle-circle crossings) the
  canonical-real storage estimate explodes (a single ~3.06 GB
  acquire_storage request, independent of work budget), so such unions
  currently fail normalization with `resource_limit_exceeded`. No prior
  governed test had two crossing circles, so this is a pre-existing gap
  first reached by swept geometry; the overlapping-U test uses
  separation 8 so every pre-arrangement vertex stays rational.
  Human/reviewer deliberation on the intended contract for irrational
  boundary vertices may be warranted.
