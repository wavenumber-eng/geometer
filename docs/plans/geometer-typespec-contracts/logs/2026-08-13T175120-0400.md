# Log: exact-backend design canonicality and budget remediation

specify total key comparison, degenerate expression collapse, unreachable-node
rejection, the 4096-bit canonical-isolation boundary, exact Thom-sign
evaluation, or charge-before-work behavior for normalization and polynomial
algorithms.

length-delimited construction-key encoding used by every sort. Sum/product
normalization now closes empty, one-child, all-rational, zero, and rational-

list. Canonical isolating precision is searched from zero through 4096 with a
precharged refinement step and fails `resource_limit_exceeded` when no interval
is available at the ceiling. Thom signs are exact selected-root signs computed
with signed remainder/subresultant methods, not interval samples.

root, isolation, and sign work now occurs in a transaction-local arena after
