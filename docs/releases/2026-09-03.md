# Release Notes: 2026-09-03

## `2026.9.3`

### Analytic planar-Boolean robustness

- Stabilizes exact capsule-chain and tangent-junction topology used by PCB
  copper presentation while preserving authored source lineage.
- Authenticates filtered source bindings before solving so malformed internal
  inputs remain contract errors and genuine solver failures stay job-local.
- Adds a narrow, deterministic recovery for same-width positive capsules whose
  corresponding endpoints differ by at most 1 um. The earliest-authored
  geometry is used without changing subtractive operands or stage order.
- Reports every nonzero recovery as
  `geometer.operation.analytic_planar_boolean.resolution_coalesced`; native and
  WASM telemetry include the count and maximum adjustment.

The general topology-resolution envelope remains 50 nm. The 1 um rule is only
for nearly duplicate positive capsules, is representative-bounded rather than
transitive, and does not merge different widths or subtractive geometry.

### Browser illustration workbench

- Adds the self-contained HLR illustration lab and improves surface fusion,
  mesh/HLR controls, z ordering, camera stability, and review lighting.
- Keeps the illustration workbench explicitly experimental; it does not expand
  the governed analytic planar-Boolean contract.

### Validation

- Full Yoshi copper input succeeds with one reported 1 nm recovery and zero
  failed jobs.
- Native and browser-WASM Yoshi results are byte-identical: 7,996 bytes,
  SHA-256 `9eefc8d66e929c38ca19046e5351638028634d01c192eeeada22625a818fae06`.
- Python, TypeScript, Rust, native, and WASM codecs accept the successful-job
  warning consistently.
