for `geometry.analytic_planar_boolean_batch.a0` while generic transport
implementation remains disabled.

the source-independent observation manifest at MATZ revision

`3c4837541105723e29e1ed26a2af56c31a893524b967c55eaaa027e0d0ca5a0a`.

integration cases. It is an input, not a TypeSpec or packet schema.

behavior for regularized point/edge contact, analytic line/circle extraction,

classification, normalization collision, absorbed-positive history limits, and

`87db47536893aa98464d81be69cd4e2dd89370cfd05f8ad825d18d19021c3de4`.

conformance oracle. The 1.25/1.4-nm collision remains a job-local topology

Authored `docs/design/analytic-planar-boolean-a0.md` and
`docs/design/analytic-planar-boolean-packet-a0.md`. They propose the logical

specification. The planar-only legacy artifact is not expanded, no dedicated
