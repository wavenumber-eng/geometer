Geometry

STEP → GLB surface mesh

Chord error: maximum positional gap, in millimetres, between the exact STEP surface and its triangle approximation. Smaller values add triangles on curved geometry; planar faces are mostly unchanged.

Mesh angle: angular deflection allowed while the mesh follows changing surface direction. Smaller angles add triangles where curvature changes and make rounded silhouettes appear smoother.

Both settings rebuild the filled 3D preview and SVG/Canvas surface mesh by reconverting STEP to GLB.

Fast vector linework mesh

Controls only fast vector detail lines and the fast mesh-shadow outline; it does not change filled surface triangles.

The demo does not impose a triangle-count limit; mesh density is controlled by the STEP and HLR tolerances above.

Surface

Experimental AO casts bounded CPU rays against the complete tessellated assembly, then quantizes ambient-accessibility values into vector-safe tonal bands. Fusion converts safe opaque triangle groups into polygon regions while preserving depth-order boundaries. Coplanar layering paints a continuous base beneath shallow material markings before overpainting their colors.

Linework

SVG and Canvas use the fast C++/WASM vector engines. Camera changes rebuild projected vector linework for the selected view.
